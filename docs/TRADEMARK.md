# Memuki Trademark Policy

Version 1.0

"Memuki" and any future Memuki logos are trademarks of Rowan van Rooijen ("Maintainer"). This policy describes how they may be used.

Memuki's code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](../LICENSE). That licence covers the code, not the name. You may copy, modify, and distribute the code under the AGPL without permission; this policy only governs whether the result may be called "Memuki".

The goal is that anyone who sees the Memuki name can trust that it refers to the official project, or is clearly identified as something else.

## Allowed without permission

- **Unmodified distribution.** Distributing unmodified Memuki releases, or builds of unmodified source, under the Memuki name.
- **Packaging.** Distributing Memuki through Linux distributions, package managers, and similar channels under the Memuki name, with changes limited to packaging, build configuration, porting, and security or bug fixes.
- **Referring to the project.** Using the name to refer to Memuki factually: in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, videos, and courses about Memuki; and in statements such as "compatible with Memuki", "for Memuki", or "based on Memuki".
- **Decks and data.** Describing decks, templates, dictionaries, and other content as made for Memuki, such as "Japanese vocabulary deck for Memuki".
- **Community spaces.** Using the name for user groups, forums, chat servers, and social media accounts about Memuki, provided they are clearly identified as unofficial.
- **Self-hosting.** Running a Memuki sync server for yourself, your family, or your organization's internal use.

In all of these uses, do not suggest that the Maintainer endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with you or your work unless that is true.

## Requires permission

- **Modified versions.** Distributing a version with changes beyond those listed under *Packaging* under the Memuki name. Modified versions are welcome under a different name. You may describe them as "based on Memuki".
- **Public services.** Offering a sync server or other hosted service to the public under a name that contains "Memuki".
- **Product and company names.** Using "Memuki", or a confusingly similar name, as part of the name of a product, app, service, company, or domain name.
- **Commercial goods.** Selling merchandise, or selling decks, courses, or other products whose name contains "Memuki".
- **Logos.** Using Memuki logos, except unmodified to link to or refer to the official project.

## Not allowed

- Using the name or logos in a way that is misleading, or that suggests your product is the official Memuki.
- Registering "Memuki" or a confusingly similar name as a trademark, company name, or domain name.
- Using the name or logos for malware, phishing, or imitations of the official app in app stores.

## Requesting permission

Open an issue on the [Memuki repository](https://github.com/rowan-vr/memuki/issues) describing the intended use. Permission is given in writing and may be limited or withdrawn.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is the one in the `main` branch of the Memuki repository.
